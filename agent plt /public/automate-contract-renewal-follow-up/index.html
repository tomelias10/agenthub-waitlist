Frequently asked questions

How far in advance does it start renewal follow-up?

You set the lead time — most clients start outreach 60-90 days before a contract's renewal date, with reminders continuing until it's resolved.

Can it flag which accounts are at risk of not renewing?

It surfaces accounts that haven't responded to renewal outreach so your team can step in personally before the deadline.

Does it handle the actual renewal negotiation?

No — it handles the follow-up and scheduling. Anything involving pricing or negotiation is routed to your team.
