Why this brings warmer leads

Lesson scheduling for a driving school depends on matching student availability against several instructors' calendars at once, and that coordination breaks down fast without a system tracking it.

A driving school with several instructors on the road at any given time has almost no one free to answer new inquiries as they come in, so a package question from a parent often sits until an instructor is back between lessons. Coordinating a reschedule means checking which instructor and vehicle are free at a new time, which by phone or text can take several exchanges. Test dates add a hard deadline that's easy to lose track of across dozens of students, and a missed reminder means a wasted test slot.
