Why this brings warmer leads

Without someone dedicated to procurement, vendor follow-up only happens when a delay becomes urgent enough to notice — by which point the delay has already cost time.

A business without a procurement team sends purchase orders and then hopes for the best, since no one has the bandwidth to check in on every open order until something is clearly late. Delivery delays usually surface only when a customer or internal team asks where something is, well after it should have arrived.
