Why this brings warmer leads

Movers compete almost entirely on responsiveness and clarity at the quote stage — the first company to give a confident answer usually books the job.

Moving is one of the most quote-shopped services in local business, with most customers requesting estimates from three to five companies within the same day. The mover who replies first with a clear, confident quote — and follows up without being pushy — wins a disproportionate share of jobs. Orynval's AI operations employee handles the initial intake questions, sends the follow-up that keeps a lead warm, and confirms move-day logistics so the booking team can focus on the moves that are ready to close.
