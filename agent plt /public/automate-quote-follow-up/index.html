Why this brings warmer leads

A quote that goes unanswered for a week is usually a lost deal, not a slow one — most buyers move on to whoever follows up, not whoever quoted lowest.

Sending a quote or estimate is the easy part — the deals that actually close usually come down to who follows up, and when. In most service businesses, that follow-up depends entirely on someone remembering to check a spreadsheet or their inbox days later, which means plenty of quotes simply go quiet.
